## 3. Decisions the team should discuss

1. **Is gradient boosting still the final model?** On validation it has the best PR-AUC (0.748) but is **no longer clearly better than the baseline** (range includes 0), and it ties with the network (+0.001). On the test it has the **lowest** PR-AUC of the three (0.702) and an F1 **below the grid**.
   - Options: (a) keep gradient boosting (still the best on validation, the same model every time, and exact SHAP explanations) and reword 10.6 honestly; (b) pick logistic regression (simpler, about as good, and no clear difference).
   - **Do not switch because of the test:** the choice must come from validation, and the report should say so.
2. **Gradient boosting's F1 threshold is now 0.452** (it was 0.360). It's chosen on validation, where it gave the best F1. On the test it gives low recall (61% of podiums found), which is why the test F1 is low. That's a legitimate result, but it should be explained in 10.9 / 10.10 (the threshold that was best on validation doesn't carry over well).
3. **The network's exact numbers depend on the computer.** The final run should be on **Kaggle**, and the network sentences checked against that run (10.4 already warns about this).
